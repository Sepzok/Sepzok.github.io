Delete your Sepzok account

This page is for the Sepzok app (Google Play listing name: Sepzok / 随造), published by Shenzhen Keluosaisi Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.

How to request account deletion

Use either path below. Both delete the same overseas Sepzok account.

  1. On this page: enter the email and password for your Sepzok account, tap Review deletion, then confirm Delete account.
  2. In the app: open Sepzok → Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm.

Data that is deleted

  • Sign-in credentials (email and password hash)
  • Account profile (display name, plan, and related account settings)
  • Usage counters tied to the account (including official AI daily limits)
  • Optional diagnostics or feedback you submitted while signed in
  • Sale / share opt-out preference stored on the account

Data that is kept

  • Light apps and files that stay only on your device are not removed by this request. Delete them in the app or uninstall Sepzok if you want them gone from the device.
  • Official AI prompts are processed to produce that reply and are not kept as an account browsing history. They are not restored as part of your account after deletion.

Retention

After deletion completes, the overseas account and the server-side data listed above are removed. There is no additional retention period for that account record. The action cannot be undone.
