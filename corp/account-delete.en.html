Delete your Sepzok account

After deletion, your account and usage records are removed and cannot be restored.

  1. Open the installed Sepzok app
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

In-app deletion uses the same process as this page. If Sepzok is not installed, install it, sign in, then follow the steps above.
