Do Not Sell or Share

Sepzok does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can still record an opt-out preference on your overseas account. We honour Global Privacy Control (GPC) the same way.

The preference is stored on the account. Product paths that would sell or share for advertising stay off. Optional diagnostics stay off if you also clear them on the privacy rights page.
